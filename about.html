An HR leader who understands both sides of the desk.

Her story

People-first HR, with practical systems.

Arifa has led HR across financial services, IT, retail, electrical and mechanical contracting, education, and beauty and wellness, supporting multi-country teams and aligning people strategy with business goals.

She combines strategic HR partnering with a people-first approach: implementing HR systems, developing policies and leading organisational change, while keeping compliance and engagement in view.

As a Certified Transformation Life Coach she brings empathy, coaching and development into HR practice, helping employees and leaders unlock their strengths and perform at their best.
